# Retail Sales & Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales and customer transaction data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and store-level trends.

The project was developed using Microsoft Excel and Power BI, with a focus on data cleaning, validation, analysis, and business-focused reporting.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw retail transaction data into meaningful insights that can support business decisions around:

- Sales performance
- Product performance
- Profitability
- Customer behavior
- Store performance
- Returns
- Discounts


## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft Power BI

## Project Workflow

1. Reviewed the raw datasets
2. Identified data quality issues
3. Cleaned and validated the data using Excel
4. Performed exploratory analysis
5. Created calculated fields and business metrics
6. Built an interactive Power BI dashboard
7. Analyzed the results and identified business insights

## Key Business Questions

The project addresses questions such as:

- What are the overall sales and profit trends?
- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which products generate the highest and lowest profit?
- How do discounts affect sales and profitability?
- Which stores perform better in terms of sales and profit?
- What are the purchasing patterns of customers?
- Which customers contribute significantly to revenue?
- What is the impact of product returns?
- Are there any unusual or invalid records in the transaction data?

## Data Cleaning

The raw data was reviewed for common data quality issues, including:

- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Invalid quantities
- Invalid discount values
- Inconsistent or incorrect calculated values
- Data type issues
- Other anomalies identified during validation

The cleaned dataset was then used for analysis and reporting.

## Power BI Analysis

The cleaned data was used to create an interactive Power BI dashboard covering key areas such as:

- Sales Performance
- Profitability
- Product Analysis
- Customer Analysis
- Store Performance
- Returns and Discounts

## Key Insights

The Power BI analysis identified the following key business insights:

### 1. Overall Sales and Profitability
- Total Sales: ₹341.26M
- Total Profit: ₹62.52M
- Overall Profit Margin: 18.32%
- Total Orders: 8,000
- Average Order Value: approximately ₹42.66K
- Overall Return Rate: 5.71%

### 2. Customer Behaviour
- The analysis identified 454 customers.
- 419 customers were repeat customers, representing 92.29% of the customer base.
- Repeat customers contributed approximately 99.36% of total sales.
- This shows that repeat customers represent a major portion of the business revenue.

### 3. Impact of Discounts
- The analysis showed a clear relationship between higher discount levels and lower profit margins.
- Orders with 0–10% discount generated approximately ₹177M in sales with around 22% margin.
- Orders with 10–20% discount generated approximately ₹120M in sales with around 15% margin.
- Orders with 20–30% discount generated approximately ₹34M in sales with around 5% margin.
- Discounts in the 30–40% range generated approximately ₹7M in sales and resulted in a negative margin.
- This indicates that higher discounts need to be carefully controlled to avoid reducing profitability.

### 4. Product Performance
- Tech Laptops 010 generated approximately ₹47M in sales and was one of the strongest products by revenue.
- Tech Mobile 090 generated approximately ₹5M in profit, making it a significant contributor to profitability.
- The analysis highlighted that products with high sales are not necessarily the same products generating the highest profit.

### 5. Store Performance
- Mumbai Central generated approximately ₹31M in sales and was one of the strongest stores by revenue.
- Dwarka Central generated approximately ₹5.6M in profit and was a strong contributor to overall profitability.
- Comparing sales and profit at store level helped identify differences between revenue generation and profitability.

### 6. Business Takeaways
Based on the analysis, the business should:
- Focus on retaining and growing repeat customers.
- Monitor high-discount transactions carefully.
- Identify products that contribute strongly to profit rather than focusing only on sales.
- Compare store performance using both revenue and profitability.
- Monitor return rates as part of ongoing sales performance analysis.
