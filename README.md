## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes retail sales and customer transaction data to understand sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, and store-level trends.

The project was developed using Microsoft Excel and Power BI, with a focus on data cleaning, validation, exploratory analysis, KPI development, and business-focused reporting.

The objective was to transform raw retail transaction data into meaningful insights that can support business decision-making.

---

The project focuses on understanding the following areas:

- Sales performance

- Customer behaviour

- Returns

The analysis was designed to answer practical business questions and identify areas that may require further attention.

# 💡 Key Findings

## 1. Overall Sales & Profitability

The Power BI analysis identified the following overall performance indicators:

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | ₹341.26M |
| Total Profit | ₹62.52M |
| Overall Profit Margin | 18.32% |
| Total Orders | 8,000 |
| Average Order Value | Approximately ₹42.66K |
| Overall Return Rate | 5.71% |

These KPIs provide an overall view of the business performance and form the foundation for the detailed analysis.

---

## 2. Customer Behaviour

The analysis identified **454 customers**.

- 419 customers were repeat customers.
- Repeat customers represented approximately **92.29%** of the customer base.
- Repeat customers contributed approximately **99.36% of total sales**.
- This indicates that repeat customers represent a major source of revenue for the business.

This makes customer retention an important area for further analysis.

---

## 3. Discount Impact

The analysis showed a relationship between higher discount levels and lower profit margins.

| Discount Range | Approx. Sales | Approx. Margin |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0–10% | ₹177M | 22% |
| 10–20% | ₹120M | 15% |
| 20–30% | ₹34M | 5% |
| 30–40% | ₹7M | Negative |

The analysis indicates that increasing discount levels were associated with declining profit margins.

Higher discount transactions therefore need to be monitored carefully to avoid reducing profitability.

---

## 4. Product Performance

Product analysis showed that sales and profitability are not necessarily driven by the same products.

- **Tech Laptops 010** generated approximately **₹47M in sales** and was one of the strongest products by revenue.
- **Tech Mobile 090** generated approximately **₹5M in profit**, making it a significant contributor to profitability.
- Products with high sales were not necessarily the same products generating the highest profit.

This highlights the importance of evaluating products using both revenue and profitability.

---

## 5. Store Performance

Store-level analysis highlighted differences between revenue generation and profitability.

- **Mumbai Central** generated approximately **₹31M in sales** and was one of the strongest stores by revenue.
- **Dwarka Central** generated approximately **₹5.6M in profit** and was a strong contributor to overall profitability.
- Comparing sales and profit at store level helped identify differences between revenue generation and profitability.

---

## 6. Returns

The overall return rate identified in the analysis was approximately **5.71%**.

Return performance was included as part of the overall sales and customer analysis.

Monitoring return rates can help identify potential issues related to products, customers, or sales transactions and can support further investigation.

---

# 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Focus on retaining and growing repeat customers.
- Monitor high-discount transactions carefully to protect profit margins.
- Evaluate products using both sales and profitability rather than sales alone.
- Compare store performance using both revenue and profit.
- Monitor return rates as part of ongoing sales performance analysis.
- Investigate products or stores where high sales do not translate into strong profitability.

These recommendations are based on the patterns identified during the analysis and can be further validated with additional business data.
